About Union Group

One conglomerate, one visa desk you can verify.

Union Group (UG) is a multifaceted group of companies — bringing a wide range of products and services under its umbrella ranging from Retail, Mobile phones, Textiles, Dyeing, Real Estate, Power & Energy, Tours and Travels and Hospitality. With a huge workforce, UG prides itself on its diversified conglomerate character.

Union Tours and Travels Ltd, a concern of Union Group of Companies Limited, is operating the Singapore Visa Application Centre authorized by the Singapore High Commission, Dhaka since 2012 to date; the Malaysia Visa Application Center authorized by the High Commission of Malaysia from 2012–2022; and the Thailand Visa Application Center authorized by the Royal Thai Embassy, Dhaka, 2022 to today.

We are also a member of the International Air Transport Association (IATA), Association of Travel Agents of Bangladesh (ATAB) and Tour Operators Association of Bangladesh (TOAB).

Our clients include Banks, Diplomats, Foreign Missions, NGOs, top corporate offices, and Travel Trade Partners.

Singapore Visa

Union Tours and Travels Limited is the authorized agent of the Singapore High Commission Office in Dhaka to process Singapore visas — one of the most compliant and renowned visa-supporting agents in Bangladesh, with expert, skilled hands well versed in visa formalities.

Requirements
  • ✓Vaccination card.
  • ✓Original bank statement and solvency (current 6 months of transaction history).
  • ✓Student ID card (for students).
  • ✓Birth certificate for children.
  • ✓Invitation letter from Singapore (V39 format), where applicable.
  • ✓Undertaking letter.
Singapore High Commission Instructions
  • ✓Visa fee SGD 30 equivalent to BDT 2,830 per application.
  • ✓Service charge BDT 1,300 as approved by the authority — total BDT 4,130.
  • ✓Applicants should request a receipt showing the breakdown of visa processing fee and service charge from the AVA upon payment.
  • ✓Applicants will receive a notification from the AVA on the outcome of their application.
